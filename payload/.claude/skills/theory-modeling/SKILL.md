# Management Analytical Framework & Mechanism
1. Define the core management/tax outcome to explain (e.g. tax evasion occurrence, tax audit penalty, excessive tax burden, or tax-planning compliance risk).
2. Select 1–2 complementary management frameworks (e.g., COSO Internal Control / ERM 5 elements, Principal-Agent & corporate governance, Value Chain / business-finance-tax integration 业财税融合, Fraud Triangle 舞弊三角, Deterrence Theory).
3. Build a **Dimension-to-Indicator Mapping Table**: link each theoretical dimension -> observable enterprise symptom -> required evidence/calculation -> diagnostic question.
4. Trace the causal/diagnostic mechanism chain (e.g., Governance/Control Flaw -> Tax Decision Distortion -> Non-compliance or Inefficient Tax Burden -> Financial & Reputational Consequence).
5. Formulate testable case propositions and list rival explanations (e.g., industry-wide margin compression, sudden regulatory policy shift).
6. State boundary conditions and avoid "two skins" (理论与案例脱节).

Delegation: framework design may be dispatched to `theory-agent` (one agent at a time).
