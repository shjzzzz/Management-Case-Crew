# Literature Review Workflow (Management & Tax)
1. Clarify topic, years, languages, inclusion criteria (peer-reviewed management/accounting/tax journals, official tax-law commentaries, classic case studies).
2. Search scholarly and official sources; record date and terms.
3. Screen by relevance and source type.
4. Extract question, management theory/framework, case/sample, evidence/method, findings, limitations.
5. Build a literature matrix; group by theoretical lens, tax governance/planning mechanism, or debate.
6. Identify genuine theoretical/practical gaps and competing explanations.
7. Draft synthesis with citations and a list of unverified items.

Never invent sources or treat abstracts as full-text evidence.
