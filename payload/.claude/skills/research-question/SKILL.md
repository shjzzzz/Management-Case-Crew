# Single-Case Research Question (MBA / Management & Tax)
1. Clarify the candidate case enterprise/event, industry context, and core tax/management phenomenon (tax evasion/audit remediation, tax planning optimization, or tax risk internal control).
2. Screen single-case suitability:
   - **Typicality / Revelatory value:** Why is this company worth an MBA case thesis?
   - **Data accessibility:** Are financial statements, tax announcements/audit notices, internal policies, or interview access actually obtainable?
   - **Management relevance:** Can it connect to a clear management framework (COSO, governance, value chain, business-finance-tax integration) rather than remaining a pure accounting/legal memo?
3. Propose up to 3 candidate single-case research questions, each stating: target enterprise & window, core problem, theoretical lens, required evidence, and main feasibility risk.
4. Recommend 1 primary question with a clear problem-diagnosis-solution arc suitable for an MBA thesis.
5. Do not fabricate corporate data or promise inaccessible internal documents.
